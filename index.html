beep

Data over near-ultrasonic sound (17.0–18.5 kHz), in the browser. No server; everything runs on the device.

App

Send and receive text or bytes between two devices.

Bench

Measure the real acoustic channel between two devices: mic processing, frequency response, reverb, attenuation vs distance, Doppler, audibility and modem BER/PER.

Microphone access needs HTTPS (or localhost). On iOS, tap a button before any sound can play.
